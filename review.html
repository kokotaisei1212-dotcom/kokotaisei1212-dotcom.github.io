What STK receives

STK never receives the conversation. The API refuses a call that carries any field other than these (422).

For each call to POST /v1/ask: the conversation's id, as your code sends it; the intent; the trigger (one of five words); and, if your code sends them, your AI's name or version and whether a person has joined. Nothing else: no customer id, no confidence number, no channel, no language.

At signup: the email address the key is sent to. STK keeps it for the key and the invoice, until 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask. Keys are kept only as a hash, as long.

STK keeps no row of an ask: each answer carries its row and STK's signature of it, and you keep them. STK keeps, for each day of your account, its counts (permissions, people joining, escalate), for 7 years. Each month's bill and each report is deleted 7 years after it was written. Your account (the address, the Stripe customer id, the hashed keys) is deleted 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask (or after it was made, if it never made one), billed or not: an account in use stays. GET /v1/ledger gives your days' counts at any time. STK does not encrypt the rows itself. What a row holds

conversation_id is a random id your code makes for each conversation. Do not derive it from your customer's information (a name, an email address, a phone number, an account number). The table from these ids to your conversations stays with you only. With ids made this way your rows hold no data about your customers, and STK keeps no row at all.

Health information

STK's intent ids say only the kind of request (request.status, claim.new, benefit.denial), never what it is about. Send these ids and keep your own names, and the table from yours to these, on your side. A name that is not one of these ids is answered escalate, and kept as sent.

What your code sends as the intent or the conversation's id comes back in your row as sent; STK keeps only counts. Those are the ways health information could reach STK: never put a person's health, a treatment or a condition in them.
