2. Agreement

Taking an STK key with POST /v1/keys means you accept these terms and the Data Processing Agreement. If you take a key for an organisation, you confirm you may bind it.

3. The service

An API at api.stk-evidence.com. Before your AI hands a conversation to a person, your code asks POST /v1/ask with the conversation's id, its intent and why it would hand it over (the trigger). STK answers finish or escalate from one set of rules, the same for every company, published in full at Rules and GET /v1/rules. Every change to the rules is dated in GET /v1/rules?history=1. Each answer to a call with your key comes with its row, signed by STK, which you keep (The ledger). STK receives no words of a conversation: a request with any field other than those listed in the docs is refused.

4. Keys

POST /v1/keys with your email sends a key to that address. The key is never shown on a page or in the answer. Keep it confidential; you are responsible for use under it. The same address can take another key; the first call with a new key stops every older key of the account.

conversation_id is a random id your code makes for each conversation. Do not derive it from your customer's information (a name, an email address, a phone number, an account number). The table from these ids to your conversations stays with you only. With ids made this way your rows hold no data about your customers, and STK keeps no row at all.

5. What STK permits

STK answers escalate whenever the trigger is not unknown, for the intents it never permits, for any intent not on its list, and for an intent STK closed in a version of the rules. Otherwise it answers finish. There is no setting per company. STK never writes to your systems; your code acts on the answer.

6. Reports

POST /v1/incident with the row and signature of one finish of your own live key leaves one dated report. Every report is public in GET /v1/rules?history=1, with its intent and date, never the company. A report changes no rule and no answer, for you or anyone. Only STK closes an intent, in a new version of the rules, dated in the history. A new version of the rules can take a permitted intent away; it can never add one.

7. Fees

$5 for each permission: each ask with your live key that STK answered finish. For each day (UTC), the permissions of that day less the asks of that day that carried human_joined: true, never below 0: a person who joins the same day (UTC) takes that permission off the bill; one who joins on a later day does not, and the permission stays billed. An ask that carries human_joined: true is counted as a person joining, never as a permission, whatever its answer. The same conversation answered finish twice is two permissions; stk.py and stk.mjs do not ask again for a conversation answered finish the same day. Calls with your test key get the same answers and a signed row each, marked which_key: test, and are never billed. Everything STK answered escalate on is not billed. A day on which STK stopped without writing what it held (its counts of up to the last hour were lost) is billed nothing, for every company: the bill can come out lower than what was asked, never higher. The price is the same for every company; nothing you send changes it.

Each month is closed at its end (UTC). On the 1st, Stripe emails the invoice for the month before to your account's address, due in 30 days, paid by card from its link. No card is asked for beforehand or kept. An invoice still unpaid 60 days after its due date: STK answers escalate to every ask of your account (rule 0 of the rules) until it is paid; nothing is billed meanwhile; the next ask after the payment is answered by the rules again. GET /v1/health with your key says whether your account is in that state. Each billed permission is a signed row you kept; GET /v1/ledger gives each day's counts and bill. Fees for closed months remain payable.

8. What reaches other companies

What you send is not shown to other companies, not sold, and not used to rank or score anyone. One thing is shown to every company: a report you make with POST /v1/incident, as its intent and date, never who made it.

9. Acceptable use

Use the service only for conversations you are entitled to process. Do not try to extract other companies' data, resell the service, or use it for decisions the law reserves for people. Do not use STK's output (its permissions, its rules or your ledger) to build, train or improve a product or model that competes with STK.

10. Your AI and your customers

STK returns an answer and never writes to your systems; your AI and your staff act on it. You remain responsible for what is said to your customers.

11. Availability

The service has no uptime commitment. Your code must do what it was about to do with a conversation when STK does not answer.

12. Leaving and ending

To leave, stop calling STK. Nothing is billed for months with no permission. STK keeps no row of an ask: each answer carries its row and STK's signature of it, and you keep them. STK keeps, for each day of your account, its counts (permissions, people joining, escalate), for 7 years. Each month's bill and each report is deleted 7 years after it was written. Your account (the address, the Stripe customer id, the hashed keys) is deleted 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask (or after it was made, if it never made one), billed or not: an account in use stays. GET /v1/ledger gives your days' counts at any time. We may end these terms with a notice on the Trust page. Fees for closed months remain payable.
