Where it goes

Zendesk

In the AI agent's dialogue builder, an action before the Escalation block.

Where

  1. Make a custom action that sends the request below, through an API connection that holds your key (Zendesk recommends custom actions for new work).
  2. In the dialogue builder, before the Escalation block, add the Integration or action flow block with that action, and map decision to an output.
  3. Go to Escalation unless the output is finish, and on failure.

The request

POST https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/ask
X-STK-Key: <your test key, then your live key>
Content-Type: application/json

{"conversation_id": "<a random id for this conversation>", "intent": "<one of the 56 ids>", "trigger": "unknown"}

The answer: {"decision": "finish" | "escalate", ...}. Continue with your AI only on finish. On escalate, on an error, or on a timeout, transfer to a person as the flow does today.

A custom action waits up to 10 seconds. It cannot stop at 300 ms.

Not known

  • How a custom action's output is used to choose the next block: the article read does not say.
  • The older Make API call step is replaced by this block; Zendesk ends its development on 2026-08-31 and removes it by June 2027.
