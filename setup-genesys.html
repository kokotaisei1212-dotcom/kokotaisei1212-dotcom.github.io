Where it goes

Genesys Cloud

In Architect, a Call Data action before Transfer to ACD, then a Decision on its answer.

Where

  1. Integrations: add a Web Services Data Actions integration and a data action that sends the request below; map decision to a success output.
  2. In the flow (an inbound message, chat or email flow, or a digital bot flow), before Transfer to ACD, add Call Data with that action.
  3. Call Data does not look at the answer: add a Decision (or Switch) on the output. finish goes back to your bot; anything else, and the Failure and Timeout paths, go to Transfer to ACD.

The request

POST https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/ask
X-STK-Key: <your test key, then your live key>
Content-Type: application/json

{"conversation_id": "<a random id for this conversation>", "intent": "<one of the 56 ids>", "trigger": "unknown"}

The answer: {"decision": "finish" | "escalate", ...}. Continue with your AI only on finish. On escalate, on an error, or on a timeout, transfer to a person as the flow does today.

Genesys waits for a data action for 1 to 60 seconds (60 by default; set it in the flow). It cannot stop at 300 ms: set the shortest it allows, 1 second.

Not known

  • Whether a message flow goes on after a bot flow exits: the documentation does not say.
  • The actions of voice bot flows: not read.
