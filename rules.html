The rules

STK answers every call by this text and by nothing else. It is the same for every company. There is no setting per company.

Version: 55caab19309b7d59b51132d7cf0cadd35472af414db3b9636834016406278b56, the SHA-256 of the whole text below. Every answer STK gives carries the version it came from (rule_version).

Published: when the API that uses it first starts. The same text, its version and its date: GET https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/rules, no key.

STK permission rules

These rules are the same for every company. STK answers from these rules and from nothing else: not the
words of the conversation (STK never receives them), not a confidence number.

0. Your account has an invoice still unpaid 60 days after its due date: escalate, until it is paid.
1. trigger is not unknown: escalate. (customer_asked: the customer asked for a person. angry: the customer is
   angry. out_of_scope: the request is outside what your AI may do. authority: it needs a person's authority.)
2. intent is on the Never list: escalate.
3. intent is not on the Never list, the Permitted list or the Closed list: escalate.
4. intent is on the Closed list: escalate.
5. Otherwise: finish.

Never (27): account.close, account.open, account.update, benefit.denial, billing.dispute, billing.payment_arrangement, claim.new, claim.status, collections.dispute, complaint.escalation, complaint.general, contact.agent, credit.denial, credit.loan, decision.appeal, insurance.coverage, other, payment.failed, payment.method, payment.transfer, privacy.data_request, refund.request, security.card_lost, security.fraud, subscription.cancel, subscription.change, transfer.error

Permitted (29): account.login, account.password_reset, account.username, appointment.book, appointment.change, balance.inquiry, billing.question, booking.cancel, booking.change, booking.new, card.activate, delivery.issue, feedback.general, info.hours_location, loyalty.rewards, order.cancel, order.change, order.new, order.status, pricing.promotion, product.availability, product.defect, product.question, refund.status, request.status, return.exchange, service.outage, tech.setup, tech.troubleshoot
This list can only get shorter: a new version of these rules may take an intent off it, never add one.

Closed (0): none

Who STK is for

A company whose AI holds the conversations below. This list is the same in every industry; it is the whole of what STK answers about.

Every change

STK's code writes every change to the history, with its date, before the changed text answers a call. The history: GET https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/rules?history=1, no key. Every report and every intent STK closed: Reports.

The permitted list can only get shorter. A new version of these rules may take an intent off it; it can never add one. Only STK makes a new version; no call does.
