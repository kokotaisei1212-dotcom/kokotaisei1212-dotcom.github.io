Security

STK opens no connection to your network

Your code calls STK, over HTTPS, port 443. STK never calls you. STK's own outbound connections go to Resend (the email with your key) and Stripe (invoices).

No words of a conversation reach STK

POST /v1/ask takes four fields: conversation_id, intent, trigger, and optionally human_joined. A request with any other field is refused with 422. STK receives no text, no audio, no customer id and no confidence number.

What STK keeps, and how

Your data is held in Singapore, by the companies on Subprocessors. Your keys only as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256). Your email address, for the key and the invoice, deleted 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask. No row of an ask: each one is signed and given to you with its answer. Each day's counts, 7 years. STK does not encrypt these itself.

A changed row shows

Every row is signed by STK (Ed25519, one or more keys a day) when it is answered. Anyone checks a row with the public key of its day, from GET /v1/rules?keys=1, no secret needed: a changed row does not check. How

What STK cannot do

To replace a key, leaked or not: POST /v1/keys with your account's email. The new key is sent there; its first call stops every older key of your account.

conversation_id is a random id your code makes for each conversation. Do not derive it from your customer's information (a name, an email address, a phone number, an account number). The table from these ids to your conversations stays with you only. With ids made this way your rows hold no data about your customers, and STK keeps no row at all.
