Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

STK is provided by Taisei Izawa (井澤大成), an individual in Toyama, Japan.

Contact: LinkedIn.
Email: izatai12@icloud.com

2. Your account

When you take a key we keep your email address, the time the account was made, and later your Stripe customer id. The key itself is not kept: only a keyed hash of it (HMAC-SHA256). We use the address to send you the key and the invoice. We do not send marketing email.

To limit how many keys are taken, STK keeps the IP address of each POST /v1/keys for one hour, and writes it nowhere.

When your key reports an incident (POST /v1/incident), we keep which account reported it, for 7 years. The public history shows the intent and the date, not who reported it.

3. Your customers' conversations

For these we act as your processor under the DPA. STK receives, for each ask: the conversation id, the intent, the trigger, and, if you send it, whether a person joined. No words of a conversation, no customer id, no confidence number: a request with any other field is refused. The conversation id and the intent come back in your row as you send them, so never put a person's name, contact details or health in them. STK keeps no row of an ask: each one comes back with its answer, signed, for you to keep (The ledger); STK keeps only each day's counts.

4. This website

The site is hosted on Vercel. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

5. Who else sees data

The companies on Subprocessors, and Google Fonts for website visitors. Nobody else.

6. How long

STK keeps no row of an ask: each answer carries its row and STK's signature of it, and you keep them. STK keeps, for each day of your account, its counts (permissions, people joining, escalate), for 7 years. Each month's bill and each report is deleted 7 years after it was written. Your account (the address, the Stripe customer id, the hashed keys) is deleted 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask (or after it was made, if it never made one), billed or not: an account in use stays. GET /v1/ledger gives your days' counts at any time. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Your rows are with you: each answer brought its row and STK's signature, and stk.py or stk.mjs kept them. conversation_id is a random id your code makes for each conversation. Do not derive it from your customer's information (a name, an email address, a phone number, an account number). The table from these ids to your conversations stays with you only. With ids made this way your rows hold no data about your customers, and STK keeps no row at all.

8. Changes

We post changes on this page with a new date.
