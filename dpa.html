Data Processing Agreement

Version of November 17, 2026

1. Parties and scope

Controller: the person or organisation that took the STK key ("you").
Processor: Taisei Izawa (井澤大成), 富山県富山市燕の3-14, Toyama, Japan, LinkedIn ("we").

Contact: LinkedIn.
Email: izatai12@icloud.com

This agreement forms part of the Terms of Service and applies from the moment a key is taken until your account is deleted. Where it conflicts with the Terms on personal data, this agreement prevails.

2. The processing

PurposeTo answer your code, for each ask, finish or escalate from the published rules; to sign each answer's row, which you keep; to keep each day's counts; and to count the conversations STK permitted, for the bill.
Data subjectsThe customers who contact you, only through what your code puts in the fields below.
Personal dataNone is asked for. STK receives, per ask: the conversation id, the intent, the trigger, and optionally whether a person joined. These are kept as you send them; if your conversation ids or intent names identify a person, that is personal data STK keeps. No text, no audio, no customer id: a request with any other field is refused.
Special categoriesNot intended. Do not put health or other special-category data in any field.
DurationRows of asks: none kept by STK. Each day's counts: 7 years. Each bill and report: deleted 7 years after it was written. The account: deleted 7 years after its last ask.

3. Your instructions

We process the data only on your documented instructions: your API calls. If we think an instruction breaks the law we tell you.

4. Confidentiality

Only the processor has access to the systems and the data. The sub-processors in section 6 hold it only to run the service. Nobody else is given access.

5. Security

  • TLS on every request.
  • Keys kept only as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256).
  • Each account sees only its own days' counts, by its key.
  • Each row is signed by STK when it is answered; a changed row no longer checks with STK's public key.
  • STK does not encrypt the rows itself.

6. Sub-processors

You authorise the sub-processors listed on Subprocessors. Before adding or replacing one we update that list with a new date at least 14 days ahead. If you object you may leave (section 9).

7. Helping you with requests

Your rows are with you: each answer brought its row and STK's signature, and stk.py or stk.mjs kept them. conversation_id is a random id your code makes for each conversation. Do not derive it from your customer's information (a name, an email address, a phone number, an account number). The table from these ids to your conversations stays with you only. With ids made this way your rows hold no data about your customers, and STK keeps no row at all.

8. Personal data breach

We post it on the Trust page without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting your data, with what we know and what we are doing.

9. End of processing

STK keeps no row of an ask: each answer carries its row and STK's signature of it, and you keep them. STK keeps, for each day of your account, its counts (permissions, people joining, escalate), for 7 years. Each month's bill and each report is deleted 7 years after it was written. Your account (the address, the Stripe customer id, the hashed keys) is deleted 7 years after your last call to POST /v1/ask (or after it was made, if it never made one), billed or not: an account in use stays. GET /v1/ledger gives your days' counts at any time. Stripe keeps its payment records as the law requires.

10. Information and audit

We answer written questions about this processing. GET /v1/ledger shows your account's days' counts; STK holds no row of an ask. GET /v1/rules?history=1 shows every rule used, with its date.

11. Law and courts

This agreement is governed by the laws of Japan. The Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance.
