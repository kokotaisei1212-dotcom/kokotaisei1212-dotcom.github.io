Where it goes

Intercom Fin

In Workflows, a branch on Escalation after Let Fin answer, with a data connector that calls STK.

Where

  1. A data connector (using API) that sends the request below, and saves decision to a conversation data attribute.
  2. In the workflow, after Let Fin answer, add Branches with the condition Escalation; in it, the data connector step.
  3. Workflows branch only on whether the connector succeeded or failed: the branch on finish is a later step on the saved attribute.

The request

POST https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/ask
X-STK-Key: <your test key, then your live key>
Content-Type: application/json

{"conversation_id": "<a random id for this conversation>", "intent": "<one of the 56 ids>", "trigger": "unknown"}

The answer: {"decision": "finish" | "escalate", ...}. Continue with your AI only on finish. On escalate, on an error, or on a timeout, transfer to a person as the flow does today.

A data connector has 3 seconds to connect and 30 to answer, and cannot be set shorter. Fin does not retry.

Not known

  • Whether a workflow can give the conversation back to Fin after the Escalation branch, which a finish needs: the documentation does not say.
