Docs

The rules

Each of 83 actions is on one side: completed without human review, or human review required.

An action goes to a person only where a law names one, or a trigger happened.

One text for every company, signed, fixed by its SHA-256.

The rules

The institution

The rules are approved by STK's operator; no company and no call changes them.

Additions take effect the day they are made; restrictions only on April 15 and October 15, announced 30 days before; a law or a court decision is the one exception.

No version is deleted: replaced and withdrawn ones stay published in full.
